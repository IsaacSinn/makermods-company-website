[ THE MISSING MIDDLE · 02 ]

Built beyond
hobby servos.

SO-101 made robot learning accessible. Maker Arm is the next step, with stronger actuation and a complete control system. Its printed structure is easy to understand and change.

It fills the space between entry-level arms and $2,499 lab hardware. You still get a machine you can inspect and work on.

[ ARCHITECTURE · 03 ]

Printed where it can be.
Serious where it matters.

The printed structure keeps the arm approachable. Its actuation and control bus are selected for real Physical AI development, with hardware safety built in.

[ A ]

Torque where the load lives.

RobStride 02 actuators power the shoulder pitch and elbow. Compact RobStride 00s handle the remaining joints and gripper.

[ B ]

A body made to change.

3D-printed structural parts make the arm easy to modify. You can reprint or revise parts as your project changes.

[ C ]

One bus, seven joints.

CAN communication keeps wiring and control direct across the full 6+1 DoF chain.
