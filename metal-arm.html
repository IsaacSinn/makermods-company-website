[ TELEOPERATION · 04 ]

Smooth teleop, two ways to lead.

Teleop quality is what decides whether your demonstration data is worth training on. Lead with a second Metal Arm and the joint mapping is exactly 1:1, with no retargeting error to correct for. Lead with the Star Arm 102 when you want a lighter, cheaper handset. Either way the stream goes straight into LeRobot recording.

4.1 · IDENTICAL ARM

Metal Arm leads Metal Arm.

Metal Arm × 2  +  Leader × 1  +  Gripper × 1

Identical kinematics on both sides means joint angles map one-to-one, with no solver in the loop and nothing to retarget. The smoothest option, and the one that produces the cleanest demonstrations.
