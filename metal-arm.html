[ TELEOPERATION · 04 ]

Smooth teleop, two ways to lead.

Teleop quality is what decides whether your demonstration data is worth training on. Lead with a second Metal Arm and the joint mapping is exactly 1:1, with no retargeting error to correct for. Lead with the Star Arm 102 when you want a lighter, cheaper handset. Either way the stream goes straight into LeRobot recording.

4.1 · IDENTICAL ARM

Metal Arm leads Metal Arm.

Metal Arm × 2  +  Leader × 1  +  Gripper × 1

Identical kinematics on both sides means joint angles map one-to-one, with no solver in the loop and nothing to retarget. The smoothest option, and the one that produces the cleanest demonstrations.

[ FAQ · 09 ]

Things people ask before buying.

3 kg is the rated payload across the working radius, not a best-case figure measured tucked in near the base. Body weight is 4.2 kg, so the arm carries roughly 70% of its own mass, which is well above what most desk-class arms in this price range manage.
A second Metal Arm as the leader gives identical kinematics and a true 1:1 joint mapping, with no retargeting error, which produces the cleanest demonstration data. The Star Arm 102 is a lighter, lower-cost handset at $199, sold as an option. You can also teleoperate the arm in VR if you would rather not have a physical leader on the bench.
Yes, natively. Recording datasets, pushing to the Hugging Face Hub, training, and running inference all go through the standard LeRobot pipeline. Code you have already written against LeRobot runs on this arm without an adapter layer.
ACT, SmolVLA, and π0 / π0.5 all run out of the box, with open-source reproductions covering training and inference plus step-by-step tutorials. Any other policy targeting the LeRobot interface will drive the arm too, and further reproductions are rolling out.
